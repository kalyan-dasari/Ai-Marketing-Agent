Distribution Tracker & Community Rules

Manual sharing workflow for Reddit, developer forums, and communities. Protects your account from automated spam flags.

⚠️ Community Rules & Anti-Spam Protocol

  • Full Disclosure: Always disclose that you built the tool or website. Never pretend to be a random user discovering it.
  • Zero Mass Posting: Tailor the title and details to each specific subreddit. Do not copy-paste identical posts across 10 subreddits.
  • Respect Link Policies: If a subreddit forbids self-promotion links in post titles or bodies, do not post links. Offer to share only if requested.
  • Engage in Comments: Stick around to answer questions, acknowledge critiques, and thank people for their time.
